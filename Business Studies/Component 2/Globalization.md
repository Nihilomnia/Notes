Globalization is the process but which the world is becoming interconnected as a result of massively increased trade and cultural exchange.


The biggest companies are no longer national firms but multinational corpos with  subsidiaries  in many countries.

Globalization has enabled the growth of large **multinational companies** (MNCs). MNCs are businesses that operate in a large number of countries around the world.


Globalization has also allowed the **movement of labour and capital** across international borders.

The movement of labour means that people are able to work and live in another country.

This is often restricted to countries that have an agreement in place that gives people the right to move to another country.

The free movement of Capital allows for businesses and individuals to **to spend money in other countries** as well as hold money in banks in different countries. This provides much more financial freedom.


## Causes of Globalization 


### Economies of scale in containerization

Shipping containers have been **standardized in shape internationally**, and there's been developments in **ship sizes & machinery/technology** for loading/unloading, reducing marginal costs of transportation.

> [!tip] 
> *This is a form of Technological Economies of Scale*




### Improvements to communication technology

The improved ability for firms to **easily connect and promote themselves internationally** as a result of the internet and improvements to communications technology.

> [!info] 
> *The Internet and Social media are factors also that contributed to this allowing more business to reach a greater audience globally*


### Growth of WTO

The increased effectiveness of the World Trade Organisation in **negotiating new trade agreements**, successfully **lowering Average Tariff Rates globally**, as well as in helping countries to open up to free trade  (trade liberalization). this increasing international specialization and the volume of trade.

By reducing protectionist barriers (tariffs, quotas, etc.), countries are encouraged to trade based 


### MNCs

MNCs are businesses that operate in a large number of countries around the world. MNCs are the engines of globalization. They don't just trade; they relocate the production.

MNCs allow for **allow for global supply chains to develop**: they break down the production process across different nations to maximize productive efficiency and minimize costs
